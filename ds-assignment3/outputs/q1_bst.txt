=== Traversals on a small BST ===
preorder : 50 30 20 40 70 60 65 80 
inorder  : 20 30 40 50 60 65 70 80    <- sorted
postorder: 20 40 30 65 60 80 70 50 
level    : 50 30 70 20 40 60 80 65 
height = 3

=== Deletion: leaf, one child, two children ===
delete 20 (leaf)         -> inorder: 30 40 50 60 65 70 80  preorder: 50 30 40 70 60 65 80  valid=1
delete 30 (one child)    -> inorder: 40 50 60 65 70 80  preorder: 50 40 70 60 65 80  valid=1
delete 50 (two children) -> inorder: 40 60 65 70 80  preorder: 60 40 70 65 80  valid=1

=== Save (preorder) to file and rebuild ===
rebuilt tree has identical shape: YES

=== Edge cases ===
empty tree: search=0 height=-1 count=0
delete from empty tree OK (root=(nil))
inserted 7,7,7,3,3 -> count=2 (duplicates ignored) inorder: 3 7 

=== Experiment: 2000 keys, random vs sorted insertion ===
sorted order   n=2000  height=1999  avg comparisons= 1000.50
random order   n=2000  height=  20  avg comparisons=   12.54
ideal (perfectly balanced) height = floor(log2(2000)) = 10
